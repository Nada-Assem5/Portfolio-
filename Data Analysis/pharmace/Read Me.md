# 📊 Pharmacy Sales & Financial Analytics Dashboard

This repository contains a suite of interactive dashboards that transform raw pharmacy transactional data into actionable business intelligence. The insights cover **financial performance**, **inventory health**, **product trends**, and **customer behavior** — empowering data-driven decisions across operations, sales, and procurement.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights by Dashboard

### 1️⃣ Overview Dashboard: The Big Picture  
> _“Sales, profit, invoices, and expiry status — all in one executive view.”_

- **Top Revenue Drivers**:  
  Cairo leads as the highest-performing region, followed by Shubra. Top-selling products include **Aspirin** and **Azithromycin**.
  
- **Monthly Trends**:  
  Revenue peaks in **May** and **September**, with noticeable dips in **February** and **December** — likely tied to academic cycles or seasonal demand shifts.

- **Core Metrics**:
  - 📑 Total Invoices: **1.795K**
  - 💵 Total Sales: **357.92K EGP**
  - 💰 Total Profit: **125.23K EGP** → **~35% profit margin**
  - ⏳ Expiry Status:  
    - **65.25%** Valid  
    - **31.5%** Expiring Soon  
    - **3.25%** Expired  
    → **Urgent action needed** on near-expiry stock to reduce waste.

---

### 2️⃣ Sales & Inventory Overview  
> _“Who’s buying what, where, and how much is left?”_

- **Top Manufacturers**:  
  **Pharo** leads in product diversity and sales volume, followed by **EIPICO** and **GSK**.

- **High-Demand Products**:  
  **Aspirin**, **Amoxicillin**, and **Azithromycin** are top sellers — prioritize restocking to avoid stockouts.

- **Payment Behavior**:  
  Majority of transactions are **non-insurance**, but **Misr Insurance** and **Noor** show growing adoption — opportunity to expand B2B partnerships.

- **Pricing Benchmark**:  
  Average unit price = **119.78 EGP** — useful for competitive pricing analysis.

---

### 3️⃣ Patient & Product Analysis  
> _“Demographics, generics, and expiry risks — decoded.”_

- **Patient Profile**:  
  Slight male majority (**1,036 M vs. 964 F**) — potential for gender-targeted promotions or bundles.

- **Generic Preference**:  
  Customers favor cost-effective generics like **Adalat**, **Diclofenac**, and **Amoxicillin** — align procurement accordingly.

- **Top-Selling Brands**:  
  - **Amoxicillin**: 167 units  
  - **Zyrtec**: 142 units  
  - **Azithromycin**: 138 units  

- **Expiry Risk by Manufacturer**:  
  **Sanofi**, **Novo Nordisk**, and **Ferring** show high revenue despite low volumes — investigate for potential premium pricing or hidden expiry losses.

---

### 4️⃣ Financial Performance Dashboard  
> _“Profitability by brand, region, and time — at a glance.”_

- **Top Profit Generators**:  
  - **Salofak**: **10.7K EGP** profit  
  - **Lipzor**: **4.2K EGP**  
  - **Venlizin**: **2.2K EGP**  
  → Ideal candidates for **promotions, shelf prioritization, and bulk purchasing**.

- **Regional Profit Map**:  
  **Cairo** dominates across all customer types (Insurance, Regular, Walk-in). **Delta** and **Giza** require targeted campaigns to unlock growth.

- **Seasonal Patterns**:  
  Profit mirrors sales — peaks in **May**, lows in **December** — plan inventory and staffing accordingly.

- **Financial Snapshot**:  
  - 💸 Total Paid: **357.92K EGP**  
  - 🧾 VAT Collected: **43.96K EGP**  
  - 📈 Profit Margin: **~35%**

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

✅ **Amplify High-Margin Brands**: Focus marketing & inventory on **Salofak**, **Lipzor**, and **Venlizin**.  
✅ **Revive Underperforming Regions**: Launch localized offers in **Delta** and **Giza**.  
✅ **Reduce Expiry Losses**: Run flash sales on “Expiring Soon” items (31.5% of stock).  
✅ **Expand Insurance Channels**: Partner with **Misr Insurance** and **Noor** to capture institutional sales.  
✅ **Optimize Stock Levels**: Replenish fast-movers (**Amoxicillin**, **Aspirin**) and review slow-movers monthly.
